The Leading Kitten

Brave · Outgoing · Sweet

Origin Story
Following the heartbreaking loss of Russ’s longtime feline sidekick, a new chapter began when he adopted two kitten siblings. The Leading Kitten arrived with a built-in scene partner and wasted no time auditioning for the starring role at home.
Favorite Purr-ductions
The Great Hallway Chase, Much Ado About Treats, and the ongoing one-kitten spectacular Look What I Can Climb!
Purr-pose
To make every day an adventure, greet each new experience with curiosity, and remind the household that joy deserves an encore.
Behind the Whiskers
Fearless and friendly, this little performer loves a grand entrance and never turns down an opportunity to investigate. The confidence is big; the heart is even bigger.

The Quiet Star

Shy · Gentle · Sweet

Origin Story
When Russ opened his home to two siblings after the loss of his beloved cat companion, the Quiet Star joined the cast alongside a bolder littermate. A softer entrance, perhaps—but every bit as important to the story.
Favorite Purr-ductions
The Sound of Purring, A Midsummer Night’s Nap, and the critically acclaimed chamber piece Behind the Curtain.
Purr-pose
To bring comfort, offer a gentle presence, and prove that not every star needs to stand center stage to steal the show.
Behind the Whiskers
A tenderhearted observer who prefers to take in the scene before joining it. Once comfortable, this sweet little soul delivers the kind of quiet affection that earns a standing ovation.

FOR THE REAL BOARD

A little personality. A lot of community.

Each member can have a photo, name, and multiple roles on the front (Board Secretary, Chief of Set Design, Master Crafter, and so on). The back can share their origin story, favorite productions and projects, their purpose at the Playhouse, and a longer personal bio. Contact details would appear only if that person chooses to share them.
